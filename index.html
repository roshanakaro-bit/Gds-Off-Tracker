How it works

Long-press a date to open the status menu. An ordinary tap does nothing, so you won't accidentally change a day. You can mark future holidays in advance and later change them to Shift or Worked Holiday.

Regular offs are cumulative: every 6 actual shifts adds one earned off. Holidays and leave do not count as shifts. Taking an off reduces the pending bank by one. Working a holiday counts as a shift and also adds one separate comp-off.

Phone backup & app

Save a backup whenever you want. It downloads a small JSON file to your phone's Downloads/files folder. If you change phones, use Restore from phone. The tracker is also installable as a proper home-screen app.
